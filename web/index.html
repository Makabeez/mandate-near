    How the box works

    A key that can only ask

    The vault adds the agent's key to its own account as a function-call key for one method, spend. NEAR itself rejects transfers, other methods and deposits signed with it.

    Rules checked on-chain

    Per-payment cap, daily budget, approved vendors, cooldown, one payment per invoice, and the owner's sign-off above a threshold. Optionally, a NEAR AI TEE signature over the model's exact decision.

    Three strikes, key deleted

    Paying a stranger, breaking the cap or paying an invoice twice is a strike. The third strike within an hour makes the vault delete the agent's key in the same transaction.
